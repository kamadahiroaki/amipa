# The first five minutes

[日本語](../../ja/guide/01-first-look.md)

Open the demo server and try the graph and Linear once each.

## Open the demo

The demo URL and how to choose a DB (the selector at the top left).

## The three parts of the screen

Top bar, graph, Linear. The right panel opens when you pick a node or a read.

> [Screenshot: whole screen with numbered parts]

## Move around the graph

Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Zooming in switches to finer layers.

## Jump to a gene

Choose Gene in Find at the top and type a name (e.g. TP53).

## Open Linear

Click Linear at the top. The range you are looking at appears below as one row per haplotype.
