# Exploring the graph

[日本語](../../ja/guide/02-graph.md)

How the graph is drawn (layers, ribbons, annotations) and how to find things.

## Zoom and layers

At coarse layers one circle stands for many nodes. Detail changes the level.

## Haplotype paths (ribbons)

Pick haplotypes or contigs in Paths on the left to draw their routes as ribbons.

## Overlay annotations

Chromosome bands, genes and regions from Annotations.

## Find

Find types: Node (node name in the original graph), Locus (chr:pos), Gene, Read.

## Node details

Click a node to see its length, sequence and haplotypes in the right panel.
