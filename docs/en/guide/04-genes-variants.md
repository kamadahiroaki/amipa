# Genes and variants

[日本語](../../ja/guide/04-genes-variants.md)

Gene bands per haplotype, what the outlines mean, and variants at the base level.

## Gene bands

Height: CDS, exon, UTR; thin line: intron. The reference shows all isoforms; other haplotypes show the one projected transcript.

## What the outlines mean

Red: possible loss of function. Orange dotted: differs from the reference but most haplotypes agree. None: not judged (partial etc.).
